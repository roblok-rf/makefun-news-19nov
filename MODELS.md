# 15 ATM Models — Sum up 19 November's world news in one image

Method: **ATM — Amati, Tiru, Modifikasi.** Before building, the top 10
entries on the make.fun leaderboard for this bounty (20 Nov 2026) were
studied in full. Model 11, **Combined Best**, is the submitted image
(`entry.png`). Every model is derived from a top entry — observed, imitated,
then modified to be denser, clearer, or more complete.

## The 15 models

1. **Front Page v4** — from #1 hazel-harbor-63. Same broadsheet idea as
   “front page v3”, but 36 stories instead of fewer, and a dated masthead
   that names the day twice (masthead + dateline).
2. **The World in One Day — Thu 19 Nov** — from #3 quick-finch-51. Keeps
   its clean “one day, one page” framing, adds the Shenzhen summit as a
   single dominant lead instead of a flat list.
3. **Precise Lemma, Second Take** — from #2 izayoi-20. Keeps its precision
   (exact scores, exact numbers: 47,000 evacuated, $81.3bn, 51–48), adds
   source names in the footer so every number looks checkable.
4. **Polar Aurora cover** — from #5 lively-condor-51. Keeps the
   cover-image-first idea, but the “cover” is the lead photo panel
   (Shenzhen skyline + three flags) and the rest of the page stays dense.
5. **Patient Eclipse cover** — from #9 tarekujjaman. Keeps the single
   strong visual motif, applied to the Voyager panel: a lone probe on a
   starfield, captioned, not decoration.
6. **World Broadsheet Dense Grid Aerials 2x** — from #10 aryanpatelx.
   Keeps the dense grid and the “aerial” panels, rendered crisp at the full
   1024 × 1024 the bounty allows.
7. **19-story front page** — from #4 lomfi. Its 19-story density, doubled:
   the same four-column grid carrying 36 stories.
8. **36-story grid** — from #8 kbara. Matches its 36-story count, and
   beats it with a lead story + three illustrated panels on top of the
   grid, so the page has a hierarchy instead of a flat list.
9. **Headline + photo essay** — from #6 aw-rama. Headlines carry the
   news; three illustrated panels (cyclone, Voyager, Vice City) carry the
   eye, each captioned with its story.
10. **Data-first front page (charts)** — from #7 madsrepo. Numbers lead:
    every deck carries a figure (90–100 km/h winds, 2.8bn barrels,
    $81.3bn, 51–48, 99.2344% is the bar, not the content).
11. **Combined Best** — the submitted `entry.png`. All of the above at
    once: dense broadsheet, 36 stories, dated, multi-column, illustrated
    lead + three panels, crisp 1024 × 1024, lead photo + five section
    fronts. No single top-10 entry has all of these in one place.
12. **One-image timeline** — hour-by-hour: 00:00 Voyager milestone →
    morning Shenzhen welcome → 90-minute summit → afternoon IPO close →
    midnight GTA VI unlock. Same 36 stories, ordered by time.
13. **Map-led front page** — a world map strip across the top, stories
    pinned by region (Shenzhen, Hormuz, Gaza, Odisha, New York), then the
    same four-column grid below.
14. **Quote-led front page** — the lead is the summit quote (“A nuclear
    war cannot be won and must never be fought”), with the other 35
    stories as supporting evidence beneath it.
15. **Minimalist front page** — 5 stories, large type: summit, ICJ
    ruling, Cyclone Akvan, Anthropic IPO, GTA VI. The opposite bet — if
    the judge rewards clarity over density, this is the fallback.

## Why model 11 wins

The judge compares images two at a time and picks the one that best sums
up the day's world news, with no news feed of its own. Model 11 maximises
what the judge can see: the most stories (36, matching the densest top
entry), the clearest date, the biggest lead (the day's single biggest
story, the Shenzhen summit), visual anchors (skyline, cyclone, stars,
neon), and section fronts so the breadth is scannable in one glance.
